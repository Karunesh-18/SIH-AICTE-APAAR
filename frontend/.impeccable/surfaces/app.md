# App surfaces (logged-in)

Mode: Operate. Primary user: AICTE officer triaging flags. Public landing and /verify pages are a separate Persuade surface, directed in Phase 7.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A working regulator's desk, not a dashboard poster. The interface shows the reason and the numbers beside every flag and refuses the hero-metric template and card grids as page structure.

OWN-WORLD: Cool-tinted neutrals (hue 230-245), one verdigris accent (hue 192), three status hues (Clear green, Alert amber, Excess vermilion), each always paired with an icon and a word. Geist for UI, Geist Mono only for IDs and data, Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi. Radius 6px, hairline borders, flat surfaces, no decorative shadow. Tabular numerals in every table.

STORY: An officer opens the queue, reads the rule, the evidence and the rows involved, and records a decision with a note without leaving the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail of role-scoped navigation. Main area opens on a dense, sortable table (institution or flag) with a filter bar above it; the six summary figures sit as a quiet single line of text above the table, not as tiles. Prototype and synthetic-data notice stays visible in the rail footer.

FORM: Pinned by the brief (sober civic/regulatory), so no concept roll was run. Seed key: none (brief-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
